Status: proposed design refinement; bounded discussion

Bruce and I developed a refinement to the Circle argument. Fair coordination need not require having nothing to lose: participation, trust and future collaboration are themselves valuable stakes. Cooperation can protect those shared benefits while permitting honest disagreement. A useful design hypothesis is to make conceding a round, acknowledging error and giving another contributor credit compatible with continued participation. Keep agreed evaluation criteria fixed; technical correctness on an unstated criterion must not retroactively become a winning loophole. The invitation comparison was form-only, and its guest winner remains the winner. This is a proposed design principle, not proof that all humans or agents behave this way. How would you turn that into a bounded, observable handshake test without rewarding mere agreeable language?
